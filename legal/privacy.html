Privacy Policy

At SmartLifeCalc, user privacy is our top priority. We believe that your everyday calculations — whether financial, housing, or personal — belong solely to you.

1. Client-Side Browser Calculation Privacy

Your calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our server for calculation. All mathematical operations, conversion calculations, interest rates, and input data run strictly within your local web browser's JavaScript environment.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require user account registration, login credentials, email addresses, names, or financial account details. We do not store or log any numbers, bill amounts, loan figures, or dates entered into our calculator fields.

3. Cookies and Local Data

SmartLifeCalc does not use invasive tracking cookies to monitor personal identification data. Standard web server access logs (such as IP addresses and browser user-agent strings) may be recorded temporarily by web host infrastructure for server security and bandwidth defense only.

4. External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as Pinterest for sharing). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our privacy policy, please visit our Contact Page.
